From shelf to spreadsheet
in under a minute.

›
Scan
📱
Point. Scan. Done.

Open the Marrow Scanner on your phone. Point it at any barcode or ISBN. No typing, no searching. Works on books, games, vinyl, movies — everything.

›
Auto-fill
⚡
Metadata appears instantly.

Title, creator, cover art, format, genre, release year — pulled from 40+ APIs in milliseconds. Synced to your desktop over local Wi-Fi. No internet needed.

Built for collectors who

You spent years — and thousands of dollars — building your collection. You deserve to know what it's actually worth. Not a guess. Real sold prices, from real buyers, for real items in your condition.
